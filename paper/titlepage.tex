\begin{abstract}
Distribution substations instrumented to IEC 61850 produce streams
classifiable at the edge, but utilities cannot pool the data and the edge is
energy-constrained and adversarially exposed. We present \sysname, a federated
learning framework and an open benchmark generator: a quasi-static OpenDSS
simulation of five feeders with synthetic event overlays, non-IID by feeder
identity and Dirichlet label skew. Three
classifiers---convolutional--recurrent, spiking and selective state-space
(Mamba)---are learned over ten substations behind a three-stage
Byzantine-admission pipeline: scale-free fidelity scoring, simulated PBFT
agreement and robust aggregation, hash-chained per round. Across an expanded
battery of $6{,}830$ run records the models trace an
accuracy--energy--latency trade-off. Under a leakage-controlled chronological
split the two dense models tie at the top (\macroF{} $0.596$ reference,
$0.590$ state-space; paired $p=0.56$), and the spiking model---at $0.7\%$ of
the reference's per-inference
energy---collapses to $0.13$; the higher $0.77/0.60/0.16$ is a random-split
ceiling inflated by window leakage, and the state-space lead it shows resolves
to the tie. The reference's \mbox{p99}
inference ($3.3$\,ms on GPU) would consume $82\%$ of a co-resident $4$\,ms GOOSE
fast-message window; the other two exceed it. Under a defense-aware adversary
the pipeline beats neither plain averaging nor robust aggregation alone: it buys
auditability, not accuracy; usable local differential privacy is incompatible
with admission. Sweeping that adversary's in-cone magnitude $10\times$ breaks
plain averaging, not the geometric median; at the endpoints honest rejection
falls from $0.80$ to $0.47$ while attacker admission does not fall. Batteries
regenerate from the committed manifests; per-run configuration hashes verify
canonically for $67\%$ of the original battery's records.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Highlights}

\begin{itemize}\setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
\item Two dense model families tie on accuracy inside a three-way edge trade-off
\item A quasi-static OpenDSS generator makes the federation non-IID by construction
\item A high-magnitude in-cone attacker breaks plain averaging, not the geometric median
\item Under a defense-aware attacker, agreement buys auditability, not model accuracy
\item Usable local differential privacy is incompatible with the Byzantine admission gate
\end{itemize}
